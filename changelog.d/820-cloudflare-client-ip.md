- **Fixed: sign-up and sign-in rate limits behind Cloudflare key on each player, not on Cloudflare's edge (#820).** With the domain proxied by Cloudflare, every player reached the box from a Cloudflare edge address and shared one sign-up and one sign-in budget. Set `CLIENT_IP_BEHIND_CLOUDFLARE=true` (`.env`, `.env.fly`) or `behind_cloudflare = true` (AWS) and the app keys those limits on `CF-Connecting-IP`, read only when the connecting hop is inside Cloudflare's published ranges, so a client reaching the origin directly cannot pick its own budget. Any value other than `true`, `false` or empty stops the app at startup. The per-address budgets are raised for a room of about 650 people sharing one venue NAT address: account creation from 300 to 1000, and sign-in from 600 to 2000, per 10 minutes.
