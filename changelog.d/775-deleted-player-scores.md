- **Fixed: a deleted player's in-flight Secure Development score can no longer restore their solves (#775).** Deleting a contestant now marks the player deleted before it clears their scores, and the scorer checks that mark atomically with the solve write: a score posted while the delete runs gets a retryable `503`, and one posted after it gets a final `422` (`player-deleted`) that sync logs as `refused`. A delete that fails partway lifts its hold on the player's scores (only its own mark); run it again to finish it. If the cleanup itself failed, the scores stay held until the delete is run again.
