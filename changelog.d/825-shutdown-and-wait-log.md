- **Fixed: the scorer and sync stop cleanly, and a slow Redis start no longer reads like the v1-data refusal (#825).** Both ran as PID 1 with no signal handler, so `docker stop` or a redeploy waited the full timeout and then killed them mid-poll or mid-score. On SIGTERM sync now finishes the tick in progress, saves its cursor and exits (at once if it is idle), and the scorer stops accepting connections, lets the requests in flight finish their score writes, and exits. At startup, the app, the scorer and sync used to log `Redis compatibility check failed: fetch failed` while srh was still starting, the same words as the v1-data refusal; they now log `waiting for Redis (…), retrying` and keep trying for about a minute, then exit with `Redis did not answer after N tries`. A real refusal still logs `Redis compatibility check failed` once and exits.
