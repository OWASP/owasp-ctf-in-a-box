- **Fixed: a Secure Development verdict judged while frozen, before the scoring start or before a master reset no longer counts after a later comment edit (#821).** sync scored a PR by its score comment's last edit time, and the scoring workflow also edits that comment without judging when a push lands inside the re-run cooldown, so the old verdict was re-dated into a live window and counted. The judge now writes the verdict's own time (`judgedAt`) into the score marker, and sync times the score by it: the freeze, the scoring window and the master-reset epoch are judged at the time the PR was actually judged. A marker without `judgedAt`, from an older scorer image, keeps the comment's time. Rebuild and mirror the scorer image (`ctf-setup.sh org` or your usual `SCORE_IMAGE` build) so forks judge with the new one; the fork workflow file does not change.
