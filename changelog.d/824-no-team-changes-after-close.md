- **Fixed: teams are final once scoring closes (#824).** After the scoring close (`scoringEndsAt`) had passed, a player could still leave their team, and a captain could still remove a member or disband, taking points off the final standings after the results were out. Those three actions are now refused once scoring has closed ("Scoring has closed: teams are final"), even when confirmed. An organizer can still move people from `/admin` → Support. Before launch and during play nothing changes. A failed settings read fails open, as the registration check beside it does, so a Redis blip never traps a player on a team.
