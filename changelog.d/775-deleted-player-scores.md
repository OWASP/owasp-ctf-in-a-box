- **Fixed: a deleted player's in-flight Secure Development score can no longer restore their solves (#775).** Deleting a contestant now puts the player's scores on hold (`ctf:score-hold:<id>`) before it clears them, and the scorer checks that hold atomically with the solve write: a score posted while the delete runs gets a retryable `503`, and one posted after it gets a final `422` (`player-deleted`) that sync logs as `refused`. A delete that fails partway lifts its hold on the player's scores (only its own token); run it again to finish it. If the cleanup itself failed, the scores stay held until the delete is run again.
