- **Fixed: teams are final once scoring closes (#824).** After the scoring close (`scoringEndsAt`) had passed, a player could still leave their team, a captain could still remove a member or disband, and a teamless player could still join or create a team carrying their banked Secure Development solves, changing the final standings after the results were out. Once scoring has closed, leaving, joining, creating a team, playing solo, removing a member, disbanding, renaming and issuing a new join code are refused ("Scoring has closed: teams are final"), even when confirmed. An organizer can still move people from `/admin` → Support. Before launch and during play nothing changes. A failed settings read fails open, as the registration check does, so a Redis blip never traps a player on a team.
