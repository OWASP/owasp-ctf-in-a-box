- **Fixed: a failing Discord webhook no longer drops announcements within a minute (#843).** A failed announcement used its three attempts on consecutive 30-second ticks, so a webhook outage of more than about a minute lost everything queued in it, and the card's red failure line then stopped saying anything was waiting. Retries now back off (1, 5, 15 and 30 minutes, five attempts in all), so a webhook fixed within about 50 minutes still delivers in order, and an announcement that runs out of attempts is counted on the card ("… N pending, M dropped") until a later post gets through.
